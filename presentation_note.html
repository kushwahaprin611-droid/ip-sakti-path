1. Executive Summary & Core Problem

The Core Problem in 1 Line: India has the world's richest Ayurvedic heritage, but Indian innovators face 25+ complex laws, unaffordable legal fees (Rs. 1-5 Lakhs), and rampant foreign biopiracy (e.g. US patents on Turmeric, Neem, Basmati).
Our Solution: IP SAKTI PATH is an autonomous, Responsible-AI navigation system that guides Ayurvedic startups from raw idea to legally protected innovation in under 2 minutes with zero login.
Key Differentiator 1: Grounded in Real Law

Every single recommendation cites codified Indian statutes (Patents Act Sec 3p/3e, BDA 2002, D&C Rule 158B, FSSAI 2022, WIPO GRATK 2024).

Key Differentiator 2: Safe Abstention

When legal evidence is insufficient, our AI honestly abstains ("Verified Evidence Insufficient") rather than hallucinating fake sections.

4. Teacher / Evaluator Defense Sheet (Tough Questions & Exact Answers)

Q1: "Is this application providing unauthorized legal counsel?"
Answer: "No, Sir/Madam. We have strict standing disclaimers across every page stating this is educational navigation grounded in codified statutes. We never issue patent certificates; we guide innovators on what to file before meeting registered patent agents."
Q2: "How is this different from asking ChatGPT or Gemini?"
Answer: "Generic LLMs frequently hallucinate fake legal sections or confuse US law with Indian law. Our platform uses a curated Statutory RAG engine with 25+ verified acts. Crucially, we implemented Safe Abstention — if the evidence isn't 100% verified, our system refuses to answer."
Q3: "What is TKDL and why does Section 3(p) matter for Ayurveda?"
Answer: "TKDL (Traditional Knowledge Digital Library) holds 4.4 Lakh ancient Indian formulations. Section 3(p) of the Patents Act prevents patenting traditional knowledge. Our system checks this so innovators avoid wasted filings and focus on patenting novel synergistic improvements (Section 3e)."
Q4: "What is NBA Form 3 and ABS compliance under BDA 2024?"
Answer: "Under the Biological Diversity Act, commercial use of Indian biological resources requires National Biodiversity Authority (NBA) approval and sharing 3.0% to 5.0% of benefits with local tribal and indigenous conservation communities."
Q5: "What is the underlying technology stack?"
Answer: "Pure, high-performance vanilla HTML5, CSS3, and modern JavaScript bundled into a lightweight, self-contained 300KB engine. It requires zero backend server, works 100% offline via Service Worker, and renders instantly on any projector or mobile device."
